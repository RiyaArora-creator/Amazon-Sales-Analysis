# Amazon-Sales-Analysis
**PROBLEM STATEMENT**

**KPI'S REQUIREMENT**

➤ YTD Sales: Monitor year-to-date sales to gauge the overall revenue performance over time.

➤ QTD Sales: Track quarterly sales figures to identify sales trends and fluctuations.

➤ YTD Products Sold: Analyse the total number of products sold throughout the year to understand product movement.

➤ YTD Reviews: Keep tabs on year-to-date product reviews to assess customer feedback and satisfaction.

**CHARTS REQUIREMENT**

➤ YTD Sales by Month (Line Chart): Visualize sales trends over time on a monthly basis to identify seasonal patterns and growth trends.

➤ YTD Sales by Week (Column Chart): Display sales data on a weekly basis to pinpoint shorter-term fluctuations and performance insights.

➤ Sales by Product Category (Text/Heat Map): Utilize a text or heat map visualization to provide a high-level overview of sales across different product categories.

➤ Top 5 Products by YTD Sales (Bar Chart): Highlight the top-performing products based on year-to-date sales to focus on key revenue generators.

➤ Top 5 Products by YTD Reviews (Bar Chart): Identify the top-rated products by year-to-date reviews to understand customer preferences.

**SOFTWARE USED**

1. MS OFFICE/EXCEL

2. POWER BI

 **Business Impact and Insights** 
 
1️⃣ Overall Sales Performance

Year-to-Date (YTD) sales show consistent upward growth, indicating strong demand and stable revenue generation across the year.

Quarter-to-Date (QTD) analysis highlights peak sales in specific quarters, suggesting seasonal buying behavior driven by festivals, discounts, and promotional events.

2️⃣ Monthly & Weekly Sales Trends

Monthly sales trend reveals noticeable spikes during high-demand months, confirming the impact of seasonal campaigns and Amazon sale events.

Weekly sales analysis shows short-term fluctuations, helping identify high-performing weeks and potential underperforming periods requiring promotional intervention.

3️⃣ Product Sales Performance

A small group of products contributes a significant share of YTD sales, indicating a classic Pareto (80/20) pattern.

Top 5 products by YTD sales act as key revenue drivers, making them ideal candidates for focused marketing and inventory prioritization.

4️⃣ Category-Wise Insights

Sales distribution across product categories highlights strong dominance of specific categories, helping identify profitable segments.

Heat map visualization makes it easy to spot high- and low-performing categories, supporting data-driven decisions for category expansion or optimization.

5️⃣ Customer Engagement & Reviews

Products with higher YTD reviews generally align with higher sales, showing a strong relationship between customer feedback and purchasing decisions.

Top 5 products by YTD reviews reflect customer trust and satisfaction, making them ideal for brand promotion and upselling strategies.

6️⃣ Business Impact & Decision Support

The dashboard enables real-time monitoring of sales KPIs, improving decision-making for sales, marketing, and inventory teams.

Insights help optimize promotional timing, stock planning, and category focus, directly supporting revenue growth strategies.

7️⃣ Tools & Visualization Effectiveness

Power BI dashboards provide interactive filtering, allowing stakeholders to drill down by time period, product, and category.

Excel was used for data cleaning and preprocessing, ensuring accuracy and consistency before visualization.

🔍 Project Value Summary

Delivered an end-to-end sales analytics solution using Excel and Power BI.

Transformed raw Amazon sales data into actionable insights for business growth.

Designed KPI-driven dashboards suitable for real-world e-commerce decision-making.
